About the data

Images: NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA): SPHEREx and WISE. Known stars: ESA Gaia DR3. Sky atlas: CDS Aladin Lite.

Images are precomputed into this site because the archive can be slow or unavailable. Frames marked "sample data" are placeholders, not real observations.
